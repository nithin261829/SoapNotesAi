## Full description

**Speak the encounter. Get the note.**

SoapNotesAi turns a clinician's spoken encounter summary into a complete,
structured SOAP note — Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan — ready to
review, edit, and print on your clinic's own letterhead in seconds.

**Built for real clinics:**
- 🎙️ Dictate in your language — English, Hindi, Telugu, Kannada and more
- 📋 Structured SOAP notes with editable sections
- 🖨️ Branded PDF letterheads with your clinic logo, address, and signature block
- ✅ Verified-doctor badge printed on every PDF after credential verification
- 👥 Multi-clinic support with staff accounts (print & follow-up access only)
- 📅 Follow-up scheduling with automatic morning reminders
- 📊 Daily and monthly case tracking, quarterly Excel exports
- 🔒 Patient data encrypted in the cloud — nothing stays on the phone

**Plans:** Pay-per-note (₹5/note), Standard (₹499/month per clinic, 30
cases/day), Premium (₹799/month per clinic, unlimited). New accounts include a
7-day free trial.

For licensed healthcare professionals. SoapNotesAi is a documentation
assistant — every note must be reviewed and signed by the treating clinician.
